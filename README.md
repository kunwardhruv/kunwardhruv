## 🧑‍💻 About Me

> *"I don't just use AI — I build things with it."*

I'm a **B.Tech AI & Data Science** student at **GGSIPU, Delhi**, deep in the **Agentic AI** space — building autonomous systems that reason, retrieve, and act. Across my projects I've gone from single-agent RAG chatbots to full multi-node **LangGraph** pipelines that take a raw dataset or document on one end and return a fully reasoned, explained result on the other — with almost no manual glue code in between.

I care a lot about *why* a system makes the decisions it does, which is why explainability (SHAP, page-cited RAG, structured LLM reasoning) shows up in nearly everything I build.

### 🎓 Background
- B.Tech in **AI & Data Science** @ GGSIPU, Delhi (2022 – 2026) · CGPA: **8.4**
- **2 published research papers** — Sports Analytics (Big Data + Deep Learning) & AI Resume Screening (NLP + Transformers)
- Hands-on industry exposure across **computer vision, IoT, medical imaging, and LLM-driven R&D**

### 📌 Open To
- ⚡ **Immediate joiner** — AI/ML Engineer & Agentic AI / LLM Engineer roles
- 🧭 Open to relocating **anywhere in India**
- 💬 Always up for a chat about LangGraph, RAG, or LLM orchestration
